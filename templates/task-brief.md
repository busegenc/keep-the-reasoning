# Task brief

> Write this in your own words **before** you prompt. If you can't fill it in, the problem isn't clear in your head yet — and the agent will happily fill the gaps for you.

**Risk level:** L1 / L2

**Goal** — what should be true when this is done?

**Audience** — who uses or maintains the result?

**Out of scope** — what must the agent not touch or decide?

**Definition of done** — the concrete finish line.

**Verified means** — the evidence that proves it (a passing test, a screenshot, a log, a trace). Defined now, not after.

**My hypothesis** — what I think the answer or approach will be:

**What would make me wrong** — (L2)
